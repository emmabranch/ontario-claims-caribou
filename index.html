How much of Ontario’s caribou range is staked for mining?

Active mining claims cover 10.6% of Ontario’s woodland caribou ranges, as of 13 November 2026. This project downloads the province’s claims data automatically, measures the overlap with each of the 14 ranges, and tracks how claim staking has changed since Ontario moved registration online in 2018.

A mining claim is not a mine, and overlap is not impact. A claim gives the right to explore for minerals, and most claims never lead to a mine. These figures show where exploration rights exist in caribou range, not where habitat has been disturbed.

Which ranges are most claimed?

The Sydney range has the highest share under claim. Missisa, which contains the Ring of Fire, has the largest claimed area of any range.

How has staking changed?

Counting claims that have since lapsed as well as active ones, area staked per year peaked in 2023. In most years about 40 to 46% of staked land fell inside caribou range, rising to 53% in 2021 and 60% in 2025.
